# Chapter lesson ("Text Analysis", two-tier)

For one chapter, video or talk. File name: `<book_slug>_chNN_lesson.md`
(talks: `<speaker>_<topic>_lesson.md`). Save it beside the book's other
notes, e.g. `Orthodox/Book_Readings/<book_slug>/`.

```markdown
---
title: "<Book>, Ch. N — <Chapter title>"
tags: [<topic>, <topic>, book/<book_slug>]
source: "<Author> — <Work>, Chapter N"
date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
---

# Text Analysis: <Book>, Ch. N — <Chapter title>

> **Scan note:** <which pages are quoted verbatim, which are paraphrase only, anything to rescan. Leave out if the source text is clean.>

## Curated Takeaways

### Core Claims
* "<verbatim quote>" — *<italic gloss: why it matters to the argument>*
<2–5 bullets>

### Supporting Evidence
* **Source:** <document, Father, council or scripture the author cites>
  * "<verbatim quote>" — *<gloss>*
<2–5 bullets>

### Key Terminology
* **<Term>**: <definition, quoted where possible>
<2–6 bullets>

### Synthesis & Application
* **Conclusion:** <the chapter's conclusion, quoted where possible>
* **Application:** <what this changes in belief or practice>

## Full Chapter Facts
*(Organized by the chapter's own sections, in source order. One compact line per fact.)*

### <Section title> (p. NN–NN)
* <one fact per bullet; quotes in double quotes, anything else marked *(paraphrase)* when the section mixes both>

<one ### per section of the chapter, in order>

## Chapter Synthesis
<2–4 paragraphs: how this chapter follows from the previous one, and the logic of its argument>
```

Rules:
- Verbatim quotes go in double quotes and must match the source word for word.
- Anything not verbatim is paraphrase. A section that is paraphrase only says so in its heading: `### <Title> (p. 44 — paraphrase)`.
- Talks and videos have no page numbers, and nearly every line is *(paraphrase)* unless there is a transcript.

Index row: `| <date> | <title> | <Author> — <Work> | <one-sentence thesis> | [[<slug>]] |`
